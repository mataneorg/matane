# Command palette

<Shot name="command-palette" alt="The command palette" />

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on macOS) anywhere in the app. Type to search; the results are grouped:

- **Continue reading:** the manga you left unfinished, with the next chapter. Choose one to resume.
- **Library:** manga in your library, with how many chapters are unread.
- **Go to:** any page of the app and its settings.
- **Actions:** **Check for updates** (new chapters), **Turn on or off incognito**, **Pause all downloads** or **Resume all downloads**, **Check extension repositories**, **Open reader settings** and **Back up now**.
- **Search "your text" in all sources** starts a [global search](/docs/guides/getting-started#searching-with-global-search).

Move with <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd>, open with <kbd>Enter</kbd> and close with <kbd>Esc</kbd>; the bottom of the palette shows the keys that work.
