# Statistics

<Shot name="statistics" alt="The Statistics page" />

**Statistics** in the sidebar shows how much you read and what: chapters read, reading time, your streak, and your favourite genres and sources, for the last **30 days**, **12 months** or **all time**.

[Incognito](/docs/guides/incognito) reading is never counted.

## Clearing

**Settings, Data & storage, Clear statistics** forgets reading time and sessions; your progress, read chapters and history stay as they are. **Clear history** empties the History page; your reading progress and read chapters stay.
