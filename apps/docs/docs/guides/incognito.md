# Incognito

<Shot name="history-incognito" alt="The History page with incognito on" />

While incognito is on (the mask in the title bar), Matane does **not** record anything about your reading: no history, no progress and no statistics. Trackers get nothing either ([Trackers](/docs/guides/tracking)), and Discord Rich Presence is never shown.

- Turn it on from the title bar or the [command palette](/docs/guides/command-palette).
- An indicator is visible while it is on.
- Things you do explicitly still apply: marking chapters read, changing categories and so on.

::: tip
Incognito only controls what the app writes about your reading. It does not hide anything from your network, your sources or your system.
:::
