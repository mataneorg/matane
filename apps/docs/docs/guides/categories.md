# Categories

Categories group your library the way you like: by genre, by how much you like it, by what you are reading now. They are optional. A manga can be in several categories at once, or in none.

The library shows an **All** tab with everything, a tab per category and, once you have at least one category, a **Default** tab with the manga that are in none.

## Making and managing categories

Open **Settings, Library, Categories** to add, rename, reorder (drag) and delete categories. Deleting one keeps its manga in your library, under **Default** if it was their only category. Right-clicking a category tab in the library offers the same actions.

## Putting manga in a category

- On a manga's page, or from the right-click menu in Browse and the library: set its categories.
- In the library, **Select** several manga and choose **Set categories**.

## Using categories

<Shot name="library" alt="Categories appear as tabs above the library" />

- Each category is a **tab** in the library.
- [New-chapter checks](/docs/guides/updates) can run for one category only.
- [Auto-download](/docs/guides/downloads#automatic-downloads) can include or exclude categories, and **Settings, Downloads** can keep the downloads of some categories after you read them.
- Categories are part of a [backup](/docs/guides/backups) and carry over when you [migrate](/docs/guides/source-migration).
