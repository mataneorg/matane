# Library

The library is the manga you chose to keep, with your progress in each.

## Adding manga

Open **Browse**, pick a source and look through its Popular and Latest tabs, or search it (with the source's filters). **Global search** searches every source at once. You can also paste a link to a manga from a supported site. See [Getting started](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-manga-to-your-library) for the steps.

On a manga's page, **Add to library** keeps it, with its chapters, in your library. The cover is saved so the library works offline; right-click a page in the reader and choose **Set as cover** to use another picture.

## Organising

<Shot name="library" alt="The library with its category tabs, filters and display options" />

- **Categories** appear as tabs ([Categories](/docs/guides/categories)).
- **Display:** comfortable grid, compact grid, covers only or a list, with an adjustable cover size.
- **Filter** by unread, started, downloaded, bookmarked, status or source, and **sort** by title, last read, latest chapter, date added, unread count or total chapters.
- The search box filters the current view; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> finds any manga in your library ([Command palette](/docs/guides/command-palette)).
- **Select** several manga to mark them read or unread, change categories, migrate or remove them. Most things also have a right-click menu.

## Progress and history

Matane remembers the page you stopped on in every chapter. **Continue** on a manga's page opens the next chapter to read; **History** lists what you read recently. To read without leaving a trace, use [Incognito](/docs/guides/incognito); for numbers over time, see [Statistics](/docs/guides/statistics).

## Chapter list

<Shot name="manga" alt="A manga's page with its chapter list" />

On a manga's page you can filter chapters (unread, downloaded, bookmarked, a scanlation group), sort them, jump to a chapter number and mark the previous chapters as read. Preferred scanlation groups decide which version of a chapter is shown when several groups translated it.
