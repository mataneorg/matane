# Downloads and offline

## Downloading

<Shot name="downloads" alt="The Downloads queue" />

On a manga's page, select chapters and choose **Download** (or download the next unread ones). The **Downloads** page shows the queue: pause, resume, reorder, retry or cancel.

Settings → Downloads:

- **Folder:** where chapters go, as `<Source>/<Manga>/<Chapter>`. When you change it, Matane offers to move what is already downloaded.
- **Format:** **CBZ** (one file per chapter, with `ComicInfo.xml`, readable by other comic apps) or a plain **folder** of images.
- **Chapters at once** and **resume the queue on start**.
- **Size limit:** past it, automatic downloads stop (you can still download by hand).
- **Download ahead:** while you read a manga from your library, the next unread chapters download.
- **Delete after reading**, right away or after a few more chapters are read.
- New chapters found by the library update can download by themselves.

## Automatic downloads

- **Download new chapters automatically** (Settings, Library, Library updates) queues unread new chapters after a check. It can be limited to manga you started, and set per category: **Include** (only those categories) or **Exclude** (never this one). The size limit applies.
- **Download ahead** (Settings, Downloads) saves the next unread chapters while you read a manga from your library.
- **Delete after reading** removes a chapter once you finish it, right away or after a few more, and can keep bookmarked chapters and whole categories.

## Reading offline

Downloaded chapters open without a connection, from the same reader. Chapters you read but did not download stay in the **page cache** for a while (its size is in Settings → Data & storage), so recently read chapters usually work offline too.

When the computer goes offline, Matane says so. Your library, history, downloads and chapters already read keep working; browsing sources needs the internet, and checks that came due while offline run when the connection is back.

## Sources that are gone

If a source removes a chapter you downloaded, it stays readable and is marked "Removed from source". If an extension is uninstalled, its manga stay in your library as "source not installed" with their downloads.
