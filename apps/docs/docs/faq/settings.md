# Settings

## Where are the settings?

**Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. Every option is explained in the [Settings reference](/docs/guides/settings).

## Do I need to press Save?

No. Changes apply and are saved right away.

## How do I change the theme or accent colour?

**Settings, General**: Mocha, Macchiato, Frappé, Latte or match the system, AMOLED black, and 14 accent colours.

## How do I change the language?

**Settings, General, App language**: English, Bahasa Indonesia or match the system. The **Content languages** setting (Browse & extensions) only decides which sources are offered.

## How do I show adult sources?

**Settings, Browse & extensions, Show NSFW content**. It is off by default and asks you to confirm.

## How do I run the first-time setup again?

**Settings, About, Run setup again**.

## How do I switch to beta versions?

**Settings, About, App updates, Channel**, then choose **Beta**. It applies to the next update check.

## Can I run Matane in the background?

**Settings, General, System**: close to tray, start at login, start hidden.

## Can I show what I read on Discord?

Yes: **Settings, General, Discord**. It is off by default, and never shows adult sources or incognito reading.
