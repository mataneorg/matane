# Browse

## What is Browse?

The place to look through a source's **Popular** and **Latest** tabs, search it with the source's filters, or search every source at once (**Global search**). See [Getting started](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-manga-to-your-library).

## Why is Browse empty?

Matane ships with **no sources**. Add an extension first: [Adding sources](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-sources).

## Why does a search find nothing?

Try another spelling, a shorter title, or another source ([tips](/docs/guides/getting-started#trouble-finding-a-specific-manga)). Searching needs a connection.

## Can I open a manga from a link?

Yes: paste a link to a manga from a supported site.

## Why does a source ask for a "verification"?

The site shows an anti-bot check. Press **Verify**; see [Network](/docs/guides/network#cloudflare-checks).

## Why are some sources missing?

Only sources in your **content languages** are shown, and adult sources stay hidden until you turn them on in **Settings, Browse & extensions**.
