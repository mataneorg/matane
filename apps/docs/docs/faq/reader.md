# Reader

## Which reading modes are there?

Single, double, webtoon, vertical and Auto. See [Reader](/docs/guides/reader#modes).

## How do I change the reading direction or mode for one manga?

Use the reader menu (the gear in the reader): it changes the mode, direction and fit for the manga you are reading. **Settings, Reader** sets the defaults.

## Can I change the keys?

Yes: **Settings, Reader, Keyboard shortcuts** ([how](/docs/guides/reader-settings#keyboard-shortcuts)).

## Images load slowly or the strip jumps

Turn on **Split tall pages**, lower **Preload pages** on a slow connection, and give the page cache more space in **Settings, Data & storage**.

## Can I read at night without hurting my eyes?

Yes: the **warm tint**, brightness, grayscale and invert filters, and a custom background colour ([Display](/docs/guides/reader#display)). They can be set per manga.

## Why are white or black borders cut off?

**Crop borders** trims plain margins off pages. Turn it off in the reader menu; your downloads are not changed.

## Does the reader work with a touch screen?

Yes: swipe to turn pages, pinch to zoom and tap as with the mouse.

## How do I save or copy a page?

Right-click a page: **Save image…**, **Copy image** or **Set as cover**.
