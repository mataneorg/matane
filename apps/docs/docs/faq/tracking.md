# Tracking

## Which trackers are supported?

AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu and MangaUpdates. See [Trackers](/docs/guides/tracking).

## Do I have to give Matane my password?

AniList and MyAnimeList use your browser's login; Matane never sees the password. Kitsu and MangaUpdates ask for your username and password once; the password goes only to that service and is **not kept**.

## Why does a tracker say "this build has no app registration"?

That build cannot log in to that tracker yet. See [For people who build Matane](/docs/guides/tracking#for-people-who-build-matane).

## Is anything sent in incognito?

No: nothing is sent while [incognito](/docs/guides/incognito) is on, though chapters can still be brought in from the tracker.

## Why is my tracker behind?

Updates are queued and retried if you are offline or the service is down. **Settings, Tracking** shows how many wait; **Send now** tries at once.

## Can I stop marking chapters read from the tracker?

Yes, for one tracker (Settings, Tracking) or for one manga (its Tracking dialog). Reading is still sent.
