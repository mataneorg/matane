# Library

## How do I add a manga to my library?

Open it from **Browse** and choose **Add to library**. See [Getting started](/docs/guides/getting-started#adding-manga-to-your-library).

## What happens to my progress if I remove a manga?

Its progress and history stay in the app's data; you can add it again later. Only the library entry is removed.

## How do I sort or filter the library?

Use the controls above the library: filter by unread, started, downloaded, bookmarked, status or source, and sort by title, last read, latest chapter, date added, unread count or total chapters. See [Library](/docs/guides/library#organising).

## Can a manga be in more than one category?

Yes, or in none. See [Categories](/docs/guides/categories).

## Why is a chapter I read marked "Removed from source"?

The source stopped listing it. Matane keeps chapters you read, bookmarked or downloaded.

## What does the number on a cover mean?

How many chapters you have not read.

## How do I move a manga to another source?

See [Source migration](/docs/guides/source-migration).

## How do I use my own cover?

Right-click a page in the reader and choose **Set as cover**.

## Is my library saved if I uninstall the app?

Yes: uninstalling keeps your data. Make a [backup](/docs/guides/backups) before moving to another computer.
