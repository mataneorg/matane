# Updates

This page is about **new chapters**. For updates of the app see [Updates](/docs/guides/install#updates); for extensions see [Extensions and repositories](/docs/guides/extensions#installing).

## How often does Matane check for new chapters?

You choose in **Settings, Library, Library updates**: off, every 6, 12, 24 or 48 hours, or weekly. It also checks when the app opens if the interval has passed, and waits while you are offline. See [New chapters](/docs/guides/updates).

## Why did a new chapter not show up?

- Library and category checks skip completed manga, manga you have not started and manga with many unread chapters (all configurable). Checking one manga ignores these rules.
- Matane only checks while it runs. Turn on **Close to tray** (and **Start at login**) for background checks.
- The source may be down or the extension out of date.

## Can I check by hand?

Yes, for everything, a category or one manga, or with **Check for updates** in the [command palette](/docs/guides/command-palette).

## Can new chapters download by themselves?

Yes, but it is a setting you turn on. See [Automatic downloads](/docs/guides/downloads#automatic-downloads).

## Do I get a notification?

Yes (**Notify about new chapters**): always for automatic checks, and for manual ones when the window is in the background.
